# Review

- Current models suggeest that $A$ and $B$ will disagree on the time at which $B$ reaches the lighthouse. This is known as 'relativistic simultaneity', the opposite is classical simultaneity.
- This is $\gamma$

$$
\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{\text{velocity}^2}{\text{speed of light}^2}}}
$$

It's super close to $1$, but Einstein proved that $\text{distance} \neq \text{velocity} \times \text{time}$ but there's an extra $\gamma$ factor, $d=vt\gamma$. That's what this whole model seeks to maintain... Einstein' does it by dilating time, here we dilate space to produce gravity.
